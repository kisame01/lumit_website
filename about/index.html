About Lumit

Senior engineers, from first assessment to handover.

Lumit Cloud Technology Consulting combines independent assessment, hands-on engineering and right-sized technology to improve performance, reduce unnecessary cost, and build platforms internal teams can run themselves.

Purpose

To make cloud and data technology simpler, more scalable, cost-efficient and valuable — from assessment and architecture through migration, modernisation, governance, cost optimisation and ongoing improvement.

Senior engineers stay on the engagement from first assessment to handover, and the scope stays the size the evidence supports. We are built to work alongside internal technology teams, business stakeholders and delivery partners.
