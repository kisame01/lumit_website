How we can be engaged

Fixed-scope assessment
A defined scope, duration and deliverable set. Best for audits, readiness reviews and decision sprints.
Fixed-price pilot
A working proof against agreed acceptance criteria. Best for testing an approach before committing to it.
Time and materials
Best for integration and modernisation work, where scope becomes clear as the estate is understood.
Managed service
A monthly cadence with defined outputs. Best for FinOps, governance, data quality and platform operations.
Fractional leadership
Senior architecture, data, cost or governance capability monthly, for organisations that need the judgement but cannot justify the full-time role.
Verified savings
Where a baseline can be measured fairly, we will place part of our fee against verified first-year savings. We are willing to be paid on the result.
