02 — Method
Six options
per workload

Every workload gets assessed against six options

Moving a server from a data centre to a cloud virtual machine changes where it runs. It does not change what it costs to operate, who patches it, or how it recovers. We put numbers on both sides and record the decision.

Workload disposition matrix — the shape of every assessment we deliver
OptionWhat it meansWhat it changesEffort
RetainLeave it where it is, deliberately and on the recordNothing — but the decision is now documentedNone
RetireSwitch it off; nobody has used it in monthsRemoves licence, support and attack surfaceLow
RehostLift the server to a cloud virtual machineWhere it runs. Not what it costs to operateLow
ReplatformMove onto a managed servicePatching and recovery move to the providerMedium
RefactorRebuild the parts that hold everything backScaling behaviour and run costHigh
ReplaceBuy the capability instead of running itOwnership of the whole problemVaries

A managed database service takes most of the operational burden off your hands — though not where you have version constraints, unsupported extensions, or consumption pricing that works against your volumes.
